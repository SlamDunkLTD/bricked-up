BrickedUp

How To Play

Tap a group of two or more touching bricks of the same color to highlight it, then tap it again to smash it. Tapping anywhere else moves the highlight. Bigger groups score far more (points grow with the square of the group). Specials go off with a single click and set off any other specials they hit. Each level gives you a set number of swings to clear the target. Empty a whole column in one swing for a column clear bonus of 250 × level per column. You get 3 hints per level, and a hint highlights the biggest group on the board.

Special bricks

Sweeper. Clears its whole row. Made by smashing 6–8 bricks at once.

Drill. Clears its whole column. Also made from a 6–8 brick smash.

Blast brick. Blows out a wide diamond around it. Smash 9–11 bricks.

Prism. Wipes every brick of the most common color. Smash 12 or more.

Iron block. From level 3. Can't be matched. Smash bricks next to it twice, or hit it with a special.
