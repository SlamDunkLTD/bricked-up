BrickedUp Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

The short version: the BrickedUp iPhone app collects no data at all. The website counts visits and game events anonymously, without cookies, and never knows who you are.

The iPhone app

  • The app does not collect, store or share any personal information.
  • It has no analytics, no ads, no accounts and no tracking, and it doesn't use the internet to play.
  • Your best score and your sound setting are saved only on your device, so the game remembers them. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

The website (brickedup.slamdunk.studio)

  • Visit counts. We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to count page views and a few game events, such as a game starting, the level reached and whether hints or sound are used. Along with each count it records the referring site, browser type, screen size and rough location (country), worked out from your IP address, which is not stored. GoatCounter does not use cookies, does not track you across sites, and keeps nothing that identifies you. We only see totals.
  • Fonts. The website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers to download them. Google's own privacy policy covers that request.
  • On your device. Your best score and sound setting are kept in your browser's local storage and never sent to us.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

BrickedUp is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects nothing and the website collects nothing that identifies anyone, we don't knowingly collect information from children or anyone else.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
